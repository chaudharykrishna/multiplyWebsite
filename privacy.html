Privacy notice.

Short version: this website stores nothing about you. No cookies, no analytics, no accounts, no tracking pixels. Here is the honest longer version.

Who we are

Multiply is a small software company based in Nepal. This website introduces the company and its product, SpotSafe 360. You can reach us at hello@multiply.com.

What this site collects: nothing

  • No account or sign-up exists on this website.
  • No analytics or advertising scripts are loaded.
  • No cookies are set in your browser by this site.
  • The contact form does not send your details to our server, because there is no server. It opens a pre-filled message in your email app, and you press send yourself.

The SpotSafe 360 game

When you play SpotSafe 360 at its live address, your scores and settings are stored only in your own browser’s local storage on your own device. Nothing is uploaded by us. Clearing your browser data removes them completely.

Third parties

The only external request this site makes is for fonts, loaded from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com) to display type. Google receives your IP address as part of that standard connection; no other data is shared with them by us.
