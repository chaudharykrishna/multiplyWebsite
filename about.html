Why Multiply exists

Workplace safety training hasn’t changed in decades. A poster. A PDF. A tired room.

The origin

Four people, one real brief.

Multiply started with one live brief from a warehouse and logistics operation. Its safety training was posters, handouts and the occasional toolbox talk: passive, with no way to show anyone engaged. So we built the alternative.

The insight

Knowing the rule isn’t the skill.

That brief kept surfacing the same truth: knowing the rule is not the skill. Seeing the hazard on a busy floor, in seconds, is. SpotSafe 360 trains the noticing, in context, decision by decision, and records the proof.

How we got here

Small steps, shipped weekly.

27 Jul 2026

The real problem

First call with the client: passive posters, a mixed-confidence workforce, and no proof of engagement. We promised evidence, not assumptions.

10 Aug 2026

Requirements, checked

The PACT analysis played back to the client over Meet, reviewed until everyone signed the same page.

3-9 Sep 2026

Demo, then the pivot

An in-person prototype demo, then written feedback on the 9th: logins, personal records, a statistics dashboard. Scope moved.

Oct 2026

Final demo and handover

The final build demonstrated working and handed over: one folder, three steps to run, and a team a working-day reply away. Next: more scenes, more languages.

Our working rules

How we actually work.

Not a manifesto on a wall. These are the four rules we argue under.

  1. 01

    We start on the floor

    We watch how the workday actually runs before we touch a line of code.

  2. 02

    Proof over promises

    420 automated checks run against every build, including a browser suite that moves a real mouse.

  3. 03

    We keep it ours to fix

    A bug gets discussed, shown to the people it affects, and changed.

  4. 04

    We say no a lot

    Every “yes” costs the last idea’s quality. Fewer things, done properly.
