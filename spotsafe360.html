How it works

From link to lesson in four minutes.

  1. 01

    Choose your mode

    Training to learn at your own pace, or Time Attack when the team wants a scoreboard. Three difficulty levels set the pace and the hints.

  2. 02

    Enter the warehouse

    A full goods-in bay in 360°. Look in any direction, walk the marked routes, and keep your eyes open. Nine hazards are waiting.

  3. 03

    Every flag teaches

    Click a hazard, or aim and press E, and a teaching card explains the fix and why it matters. Wrong flag? Same quality of lesson.

  4. 04

    Quiz, review, replay

    A three-question quick check, then a results map of what you found and missed, with your reaction time on every hazard.

Everything inside

Everything inside the experience.

  • Runs in any modern web browser. No IT request, no rollout.
  • Sign in for records, streaks and round replays, or stay a guest. Data stays inside your company either way.
  • Training and Time Attack modes, with three difficulty levels.
  • Nine hazards across five categories: fire, vehicles, housekeeping, racking, height.
  • A deliberate non-hazard that punishes click-sweeping and rewards judgement.
  • Quick-check quiz and a review map showing finds, misses and reaction times.
  • Personal dashboard for the learner; company view with averages, rounds per day and most-missed hazards for the supervisor.
  • 1v1 challenges with identical questions for both players, decided by the server.
  • Full keyboard route for staff who can’t use a mouse.
  • VR-ready pathway through WebXR. Same build, no extra software.
